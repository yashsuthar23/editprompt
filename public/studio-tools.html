V9.1 · FREE-FIRST TOOLS

More Creator Tools

Background removal, object erasing, speech transcription, voice recording, prompt sharing and a lightweight video timeline. Local image/audio processing stays in your browser where supported.

01 · AI Background Remover

Remove image background

Uses the open-source IMG.LY browser model. First use downloads model files; processing can be memory-intensive. Review the library licence before commercial deployment.

02 · Image Optimizer

Resize, compress & convert images

Resize images by width, choose an output format and adjust quality to reduce file size. Processing stays in your browser.

Choose an image to begin.
03 · Object Remover

Erase unwanted objects

Local OpenCV inpainting (not generative AI). Paint over a small object, then apply. Results depend on the surrounding pixels; large objects may leave artifacts.

04 · Automatic Speech-to-Text

Transcribe your voice

Uses the browser's Web Speech recognition engine. Availability and language support depend on your browser and operating system; some browsers may process audio through their own online service.

05 · MP3 Voice Export

Record and export MP3

Record from your microphone, then encode the recording to MP3 locally using a browser MP3 encoder. Requires microphone permission and a supported browser.

06 · Public Prompt Marketplace

Discover and share prompts

Browse prompts published by EditPrompt users. Publishing requires login. Do not post private, copyrighted, or sensitive content.

Publish a prompt

07 · Advanced Video Timeline

Arrange media clips

A lightweight browser timeline for arranging, trimming and previewing local video, audio and image clips. Export a project JSON to continue editing elsewhere. This is not yet a rendered MP4 exporter.

Add media to build your timeline.
