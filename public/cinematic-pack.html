TRY BEFORE YOU BUY

Sample prompt style

PRODUCT AD

Premium wristwatch on a dark reflective surface, precise metallic highlights, controlled atmospheric haze, shallow depth of field and a slow macro push-in. Maintain accurate geometry and realistic reflections while the background light subtly evolves. Polished luxury-commercial finish; avoid warped details, flicker and unstable branding.

CINEMATIC PORTRAIT

Confident Indian creative professional standing in a modern studio, warm rim light separating the subject from a charcoal background, subtle environmental haze and a slow cinematic push-in. Preserve facial identity and natural micro-expression with realistic skin texture and restrained professional grading.

IMAGE TO VIDEO

Animate the supplied portrait without changing identity or composition. Introduce natural blinking, subtle breathing, slight fabric movement and gentle background parallax while the camera performs an almost imperceptible push-in. Keep facial structure stable and avoid morphing, jitter or artificial motion.
