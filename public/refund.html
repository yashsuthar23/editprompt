Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how refunds, duplicate payments, failed deliveries, and cancellations are handled for digital products purchased through EditPrompt.in.

1. Digital products

EditPrompt provides digital products such as AI prompt packs and downloadable creator resources. Because digital products may be made available immediately after payment, refund eligibility may be limited once a product has been delivered or downloaded.

Refunds may be considered where required by applicable law, where a purchased product cannot be supplied as described, or where there is another valid issue with the transaction.

2. Duplicate payments

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, please contact us with your payment details. We will review the duplicate transaction and take appropriate action.

3. Successful payment but product unavailable

If your payment was successfully completed but the purchased product does not appear in your library, please check My Library and your purchase receipt first.

If the product is still unavailable, contact support@editprompt.in with your account email and Razorpay payment ID or order ID.

4. Failed or incomplete payments

If a payment fails or is cancelled during checkout, no digital product will be considered purchased unless the payment is successfully confirmed.

5. Cancellations

EditPrompt's listed products are one-time digital purchases. They do not automatically renew and therefore do not require subscription cancellation.

6. Refund requests

To request support regarding a refund or payment issue, email us at support@editprompt.in .

Please include:

• Email address used for the purchase
• Razorpay payment ID or order ID
• Product or prompt pack purchased
• Brief explanation of the issue
